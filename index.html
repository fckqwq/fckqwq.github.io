Research Experience

Efficient LLM Inference01

LLM Inference Optimization and Low-bit Quantization

MAC Lab, Xiamen University · Jun 2026 – Present

Built a unified, reproducible evaluation pipeline to study how rotation structures interact with quantization backends, comparing block_diag and kronecker across RTN, GPTQ, and SpQR.

WikiText-2 PPL 10.8440 → 10.5379

Qwen3-8B · W4A4 · nsamples=128 · block_diag + GPTQ

W4A4FlatQuantGPTQSpQR
Methods and findings
  • Built a workflow covering calibration cache, FlatQuant calibration, W4A4 quantization, and lm-eval multi-task evaluation. Standardized experimental settings to make results reproducible and comparable across quantization backends.
  • Verified that block_diag + GPTQ reduces WikiText-2 PPL from 10.8440 to 10.5379 on Qwen3-8B with W4A4 quantization and nsamples=128.
  • Analyzed WikiText-2 PPL and lm-eval results, finding that the gains of block_diag over kronecker depend on the quantization backend, informing further rotation-structure design and backend adaptation.
